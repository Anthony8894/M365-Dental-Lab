# overview

A user is experiencing some issues receving and sending emails from his mailbox. That is due to having a full mailbox.

In this doc, I will show you a few ways to clear up some storage. This will both be performed on the app and online version. 

## steps (online archive, web version)

1. Navigate to settings, the geear icon should be located near the profile icon
2. In here, click on "storage" and it will show you how much storage is being taken up and what is available. 
![]
3. one way is to empty the junk folder, however, sometimes that isn't enough. 
   1. Deleting individual emails from the inbox folder may take more time
4. what we can do is to setup a **in-place-archive**. 
   a. Keep in mind that the **in-place-archive** will only be available for users with a business premium licese and above. 
5. the archive will build and may take up to 24 hours.
   a. The in-place-archive will be accessible on the left hand side on the panel.

For context, the **in-place-archive** is set to pull emails that are 2 years and older. That can be changed inside M365 Purview, meaning the administrator or owner of the tenant will have to change it. TBC 08/10/2026
