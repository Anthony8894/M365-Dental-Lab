# overview

A user is experiencing some issues receving and sending emails from his mailbox.

In this doc, I will show you a few ways to clear up some storage. This will both be performed on the app and online version. 

## steps (for the web version, simple solution)

1. Navigate to settings, the geear icon should be located near the profile icon
2. In here, click on "storage" and it will show you how much storage is being taken up and what is available.
